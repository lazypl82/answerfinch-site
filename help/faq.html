Questions about Answerfinch

Answerfinch is a Google Forms™ add-on by Lazy Labs. You use it to email the right person when a form answer matches your rule.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

How do I send an email based on a Google Forms answer?

Install Answerfinch and open your form. Open Answerfinch from the form's add-on menu (the puzzle piece icon). Create a rule: pick a question, pick a condition and choose who gets the email. For example, when Request type is Refund, email your billing address.

Before you save, you can preview the email with a recent response. After you turn alerts on, you can send yourself a test. Follow the setup guide for each step.

Can I use conditional rules for free?

Yes. On Free you run one rule at a time across your Google account, and you can use every condition type. A confirmation email to the respondent counts as a rule.

Pro is $4 a month or $29 a year, taxes included, for one Google account. With Pro you run more rules at the same time. There is no separate Pro trial.

Which conditions can I use?

You can use is, contains, is not empty and is any of. Each rule has one condition. To route answers to different people, make one rule for each route.

Can I email the person who filled in the form?

Yes. You can send to the address Google Forms collects, or to an email question you choose. You can put their answers in the message, such as their name and the session they picked. See personalized confirmation emails.

Can I add CC, BCC or attachments?

No. You can choose one recipient per rule. You can't add CC, BCC, attachments or SMS.

Why didn't my email arrive?

Open the send log in Answerfinch first. In the send log, you see each recorded attempt as Sent, Failed, Unknown or Processing.

  • Sent means Google accepted the email for sending. It doesn't confirm that it reached the inbox, so check spam folders too.
  • For Failed, you see a reason when one is available, such as your Google account reaching its daily sending limit.
  • If you need to reconnect your Google permissions, review them in the add-on before turning alerts back on.
  • You won't send a response automatically if more than 48 hours have passed when it is processed.

You can review and resend eligible failed or unknown entries. Resending an unknown entry may send a duplicate, and you see a warning first.

Whose address do the emails come from?

Emails go out from your own Google account, so recipients can see your address. Google's sending limits for your account apply. You have no separate daily or monthly send cap in Answerfinch. You can check Google's remaining recipient count in Details.

Does Lazy Labs see my form responses?

No. Form responses are processed in Google Apps Script under your Google account and aren't sent to Lazy Labs servers. To check your Pro license, you share the license key and activation information with Dodo Payments. You don't share response content or recipient addresses in that check. The privacy policy has the details.

Doesn't Google Forms already send notifications?

In Google Forms, you can turn on email notifications for every new response. Use Answerfinch when you want an email only when an answer matches, or want it sent to someone else, such as a team inbox or the respondent.

How do I cancel Pro?

Send one email to runredteam@gmail.com, or use the Dodo Payments customer portal. Cancelling stops future renewals. Before each renewal, Dodo Payments emails you a reminder with the amount.
