Set up a rule in a minute

Open Answerfinch from the Add-ons menu in your form. No spreadsheet or code.

Your data and the limits

Your data stays in your Google account

Responses are processed by Google Apps Script under your account and are not sent to Lazy Labs servers. Emails go only to the recipients you choose; test emails go only to you.

Rules and the send log are stored with your form, and you can delete them from the form. For Pro, only the license key and activation details are sent to Dodo Payments to check your license. Privacy Policy

Limits worth knowing

Google limits how many emails a script can send each day, so sending isn't unlimited. Answerfinch adds no daily or monthly cap of its own.

Responses picked up more than 48 hours after they were submitted aren't sent automatically. The send log keeps each form's latest 200 entries for up to 30 days.
