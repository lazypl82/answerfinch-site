Get Google Forms email notifications with responses included

To get Google Forms™ answers by email, choose Email me every response if you see it when setting up your first rule. To pick the answers yourself, create a rule and use Insert answer. On Free you can run one rule at a time across your Google account.

By Lazy Labs. November 23, 2026. Steps checked against Answerfinch v9.

Get answers by email with your first rule

  1. Open Answerfinch from the form's add-on menu. If prompted, choose Start in this form.
  2. In Rules, choose Email me every response if you see it. You save one rule that sends to the Google account you're using. It matches when someone answers the required question shown above the button.
  3. Alerts are still off after you save. Choose Preview and test and check the email. If you have no response to preview, submit a sample to your test form and try again. Then choose Turn on alerts and send test. The test goes to your own address.

You need a supported required question in the first section. For example, make a Short answer question required and choose Refresh in the sidebar. If you already use Answerfinch rules on this account, use the steps below.

In the email, you'll see answers to supported questions in form order. If you have a long form, some questions can be left out to fit the template. You can use Edit on the rule to change the message.

Create a rule and choose which answers to include

  1. Open Answerfinch from the form's add-on menu. If prompted, choose Start in this form. Choose Create a rule or New rule. If you see Run this rule on Free instead, choose it to open the editor.
  2. Enter a Rule name. Under When, choose a required question in the first section that everyone answers. Choose is not empty to match every new response. For example, use a required Short answer question called “Name”.
  3. Under Send to, choose This address and enter your own email address. Set a subject such as “New workshop registration”.

You don't need to collect email addresses to send to your own address. If you want to email the person who filled out the form, see how to send a personalized confirmation.

Put answers in the message

For a workshop form, type “Name: ” in Message. Leave the cursor after the label, choose Insert answer and pick the Name question. On a new line, type “Session: ” and insert the Session question. Pick the questions from the menu so each one appears as an answer tag.

With a sample response, you might see this in your inbox:

Name: Sam Lee
Session: Tuesday morning

Choose Preview to check the draft. If you see Select a response, choose a sample response. If you are setting up a test form, submit a sample to that form first. Choose Save, then Save rule.

Turn on alerts and send yourself a test

If you see Preview and test on your first rule, open it and choose Turn on alerts and send test after checking the message.

Otherwise, turn on alerts with the switch. If the rule is paused on Free, choose Keep this rule on Free and confirm the switch. Choose Send me a test, check the answers and confirm with Send test to myself.

Why Answerfinch asks for these permissions

  • Your current form. We read questions and responses in the form you're using so you can check rules and preview emails.
  • Send email. We send your alerts, tests and account notices through your Google account.
  • Run automatically. We set up or remove the check for new responses when you turn alerts on or off.
  • Connect to an external service. We contact Dodo Payments to activate and check your Pro license.
  • Show content in Google Forms. We open the sidebar, rule editor and confirmation dialogs inside your form.
  • Your Google Account email address. We identify your account and send tests and account notices to your address.
