Send Google Forms email notifications to multiple people

In Google Forms™, you get built-in response alerts at your own address when you turn them on. Each form editor can turn on their own alerts. To notify people who don't edit the form, use one Google Group address with Answerfinch Free or run a rule for each address with Pro.

By Lazy Labs. November 20, 2026. Steps checked against Answerfinch v8.

When everyone already edits the form

Ask each person to open the form in their own Google account. In Responses, open More and select Get email notifications for new responses. Follow Google's steps for response notifications.

Send one email to a Google Group on Free

Use the group's email address if everyone should get the same message. You can do this with one rule on Free. On Free you can run one rule at a time across your Google account.

Ask the group owner to allow email posting and check Who can post for the Google account you use to send alerts. If that account is outside the group or its organization, make sure the group allows it to post from outside. See Google's group settings.

Ask members to choose Each email if they want every message in their own inbox. See Google's subscription settings.

Set up the notification

  1. Open Answerfinch from the form's add-on menu. If prompted, choose Start in this form. Choose Create a rule or New rule. If you see Run this rule on Free instead, choose it to open the editor.
  2. Enter a Rule name. For every new response, pick a required question in the first section that everyone answers. Choose is not empty. To send for a specific answer, choose is and pick the answer instead.
  3. Under Send to, choose This address and enter the group address. Write the subject and message. Choose Save, then Save rule.

If you want to enter each address yourself, use Pro to run several rules together. Keep the same condition and message in each rule and enter one address per rule. When a response matches more than one active rule, expect one send attempt per matching rule. You can't enter several addresses on a rule or add CC or BCC.

Check who gets the email

Turn on alerts and make sure the rules you want to check are running. On Free, if the group rule is paused, choose Keep this rule on Free and confirm the switch.

A test from Send me a test goes to you. To check the group or the individual addresses, submit a fresh sample response to a test form after you turn alerts on. Ask the recipients to check their inboxes. In Send log, Sent means Google accepted the email for sending.
