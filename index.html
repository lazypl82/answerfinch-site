Set up a rule in a minute

Open Answerfinch from the Add-ons menu in your form. No spreadsheet, no code.

  1. Choose when to send

    Pick a question and an answer, like “Request type is Refund,” or send every response.

  2. Choose who gets it

    Send to a fixed address, to yourself, or to the person who filled in the form.

  3. Save, turn on, test

    Preview and save the rule. Turn it on, then send yourself a test from the rule menu.

Google Forms tells you a response arrived. Answerfinch tells you what it says.

Your responses stay in your Google account

Runs in your account

Answerfinch runs in Google Apps Script under your Google account. The add-on doesn’t send your form responses to Lazy Labs servers.

Only the people you choose

Emails go to the recipients in your rules. Test emails go only to you. For Pro, your license key and activation details go to Dodo Payments to check your license.

Google's limits apply

Google caps how many recipients a script can email each day. Answerfinch adds no daily or monthly cap of its own.
