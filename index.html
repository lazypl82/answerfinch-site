Google Forms add-on

Email the right person when a form answer matches

You pick a question and an answer, and choose who gets the email. When a matching response arrives, the email goes out automatically from your Google account.

Install AnswerfinchFrom Google Workspace Marketplace. On Free you run one rule at a time, with no Answerfinch daily or monthly send cap.

What it does

You write rules like "if Request type is Sales, email the sales team." Each rule uses one question and one condition: is, contains, is not empty or is any of. You send to a fixed address, or to an email address from the response.

You can put answers into the subject and the message, and use basic HTML. You can preview the email with a recent response, and send yourself a test.

In the send log you see Sent, Failed (with a reason when one is available), Unknown or Processing for recorded attempts. Sent means Google accepted the email for sending. You can't tell from Sent whether it reached the inbox.

You can review and resend eligible failed or unknown entries, and resends are labeled. You also see Google's remaining recipient count and how many recipients Answerfinch requested today.

Your data

Form responses are processed in Google Apps Script under your Google account, and are not sent to Lazy Labs servers. Rule emails go to the recipients you choose, and test emails go only to you. Operational diagnostics in Google logs may be visible to Lazy Labs, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Rules and the send log are stored with your form. Each form keeps its latest 200 log entries, and you can view or resend entries from the last 30 days, whichever limit comes first. Expired entries are deleted the next time Answerfinch runs. You can delete a form's Answerfinch rules and log from that form.

To check a Pro license, Answerfinch sends the license key and activation information to Dodo Payments. It never sends response content or recipient addresses.

Limits

Google limits how many emails a script can send each day. You can send within those limits; sending isn't unlimited.

Responses picked up more than 48 hours after they were submitted aren't sent automatically. You may still be able to resend eligible entries by hand while they are in the log.
