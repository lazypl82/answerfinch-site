Free ways to send conditional emails from Google Forms

If you want to email someone only when an answer matches, start with an add-on that includes conditions on Free or write your own Apps Script. If you already have Workspace Studio or a workflow tool, check whether your plan covers the condition and the email step.

By Lazy Labs. November 19, 2026. We checked the linked product and plan documentation. Lazy Labs makes Answerfinch.

Use Google Forms for an email on every response

In Google Forms™, you can turn on email notifications for your own account. If you collect email addresses, you can send a copy of the answers to respondents. Use the built-in settings if either one is enough. To choose a different recipient or filter by another answer, use one of the options below.

Use Apps Script if you want to maintain the code

You can read the answer in a form submit trigger and use MailApp to send to an address you choose. You don't need an add-on subscription for that. You do need permission to run the script and send mail. Google's Apps Script quotas apply. Use the example with one form question to see what you would maintain.

Check whether you already have Workspace Studio

If your account has access to Workspace Studio, you can start a flow when a form response comes in. Add a Check if step and an email step. Check which response fields and recipient settings you can use. Ask your administrator which steps you can use with your account. See Google's list of starters and steps.

Use Zapier or Make if the response also needs to go elsewhere

Consider a workflow tool if you also want to create a support ticket or update another app. In Zapier, the free plan allows a trigger and an action. A Zap with a separate condition step and an email action goes beyond that. Read Zapier's Free plan details.

In Make, you can watch for Google Forms responses and put a filter before the email step. Check the Free plan's credits and scheduling limits against how often your form receives responses. Review the data you share before you connect either service to a live form.

Use an add-on if you want to set the rule in the form

With Answerfinch, you can run one rule at a time on Free across your Google account. If you need several rules at once, you need Pro. Compare the add-ons and check their plans before you install.

With Answerfinch, choose one question and one recipient for each rule. Choose a different setup if you need a condition that depends on two different questions or one email with CC or BCC.
