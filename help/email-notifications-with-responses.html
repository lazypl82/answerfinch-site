Get Google Forms email notifications with responses included

To get Google Forms™ answers by email, choose Send to my email if you see it when setting up your first rule. To pick the answers yourself, create a rule and use Insert answer. On Free you can run one rule at a time across your Google account.

By Lazy Labs. November 23, 2026. Steps for Answerfinch v10.

Get answers by email with your first rule

  1. Open Answerfinch from the form's add-on menu. If prompted, choose Start in this form.
  2. In Rules, choose Send to my email if you see it. You save one rule that sends to the Google account you're using. It uses a supported required question in the first section of the form.
  3. The rule is off after you save. Open Rule actions (⋮), choose Preview and test and check the email. If you have no response to preview, submit a sample to your test form and try again. Then choose Turn on alerts and send test. The test goes to your own address.

You need a supported required question in the first section. For example, make a Short answer question required and choose Refresh in the sidebar. If you already use Answerfinch rules on this account, use the steps below.

In the email, you'll see answers to supported questions in form order. If you have a long form, some questions can be left out to fit the template. To change the message, open Rule actions (⋮) and choose Edit rule.

Create a rule and choose which answers to include

  1. Open Answerfinch from the form's add-on menu. If prompted, choose Start in this form. Choose Route emails by answer or New rule. If you see Create rule on Free, choose it to open the editor. Saving keeps your current Free rule.
  2. Enter a Rule name. Under Condition, choose a required question everyone answers. Select Has an answer to match every new response that answers the question. To match a specific answer instead, select Is equal to and choose or enter the value. If you need to change how values are entered, look for Value type in Advanced settings.
  3. Under Recipient, choose This address and enter your email address. In Subject, enter a subject such as “New workshop registration”. To email the person who filled out the form, choose Respondent and use Address source to select an email question or Google Forms email.

You don't need to collect email addresses to send to your own address. If you want to email the person who filled out the form, see how to send a personalized confirmation.

Put answers in the message

For a workshop form, type “Name: ” in Message. Leave the cursor after the label, choose Insert answer and pick the Name question. On a new line, type “Session: ” and insert the Session question. Pick the questions from the menu so each one appears as an answer chip. Use the ⋮ More options button beside the message format for help with inserted answers and message limits.

With a sample response, you might see this in your inbox:

Name: Sam Lee
Session: Tuesday morning

Check the draft in Preview. Leave the response picker on Latest match or choose a sample response. If your test form has no responses, submit a sample first. The preview updates as you edit. Use Refresh to load it again. Choose Save, then Save rule.

Turn on alerts and send yourself a test

If you see Preview and test in the Rule actions menu (⋮), select it. Check the message and choose Turn on alerts and send test.

Otherwise, turn on the switch beside the rule. If you see a prompt naming the rule you want to run, choose Switch rule. The previous Free rule stops running. In Rule actions (⋮), choose Send me a test. Check the answers and choose Send test to myself.

In Send log, Sent means Google accepted the email for sending. Check your inbox to confirm it arrived.

Why Answerfinch asks for these permissions

  • Your current form. We read questions and responses in the form you're using so you can check rules and preview emails.
  • Send email. We send your alerts, tests and account notices through your Google account.
  • Run when you're not there. When you turn on a rule, Google runs Answerfinch on new submissions, even when your form isn't open.
  • Connect to an external service. We contact Dodo Payments to activate and check your Pro license.
  • Show content in Google Forms. We open the sidebar, rule editor and confirmation dialogs inside your form.
  • Your Google Account email address. We identify your account and send tests and account notices to your address.
